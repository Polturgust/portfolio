bzImage + initramfs.cpio.gz are built with demos/myshell/build.sh:
- 42sh: https://github.com/Polturgust/my-shell (branch shell-rewrite, commit c128b38),
  compiled with its own Makefile and linked statically against musl (i386).
- Linux kernel: Alpine Linux 3.20 linux-virt (x86). Other commands: BusyBox (static).
seabios.bin / vgabios.bin come from https://github.com/copy/v86 (bios/, commit 315b2b0),
SeaBIOS is LGPL-3.0 (see SEABIOS-COPYING.LESSER). The emulator is the v86 npm package (BSD-2-Clause).
